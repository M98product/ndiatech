Connect to mainnet

  1. Open your EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby…) and choose “Add network”.
  2. Network name: NODIA Chain Mainnet
  3. RPC: https://rpc.nodia.network/
  4. Symbol: NODIA. Explorer: https://explorer.nodia.network/
  5. The Chain ID will be published here on launch day.

JSON-RPC

The RPC follows the standard Ethereum API: eth_chainId, eth_blockNumber, eth_getBalance, eth_sendRawTransaction and the other eth_* methods. Ready-to-copy examples are on the Developers page.

Bridge NODIA

The bridge moves $NODIA from Robinhood Chain to NODIA Chain. A deposit arrives in about 2 minutes, with a 0.05% fee. The bridge opens on launch day.

Network settings

Target block time: 250 ms. Native currency: NODIA, 18 decimals. EVM compatible: Solidity contracts deploy without changes.

Gas and settlement

Every transaction pays gas in NODIA. Transaction batches are posted and settled on Robinhood Chain, the parent network.
